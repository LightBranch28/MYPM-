About MyPM

What is MyPM?

MyPM (My Project Manager) is a mobile app for managing projects with built-in AI intelligence. Besides tracking projects, tasks and milestones, it watches your projects, flags risks and delays early and recommends what to do next.

How is MyPM different from other project tools?

Most tools wait for you to look things up or ask their chatbot a question. MyPM is proactive: its Project Advisor brings important issues to you, with the evidence and a suggested fix, before they become serious.

When can I use it?

MyPM is coming soon to iOS and Android. Join the waitlist and we'll email you as soon as early access opens.

Is there a web or desktop version?

MyPM is built mobile first, and the app is where all the project work happens. This website is for learning about MyPM and joining the waitlist.

Do I need project management experience?

No. MyPM asks you simple questions and builds the structure with you. Beginners get simpler language and fewer settings.

AI and your data

Will the AI change my project without asking?

No. MyPM recommends actions, but any change to a deadline, assignment or other critical field only happens after you confirm it.

Who can see my project data?

Only the people you invite to a project or workspace. The AI follows the same access rules, so it never shows anyone information they aren't allowed to see.

Does MyPM work offline?

Yes, partly. Projects and tasks you've opened recently stay viewable offline, and your changes sync when you're back online.

Does MyPM connect to Jira, Asana, Slack or other tools?

Not at launch. Integrations are on our roadmap, and Founding Community feedback will help decide which ones come first.

Plans and early access

Is there a free plan?

Yes. The Free plan includes projects, tasks, milestones and a limited version of MyPM's intelligence. See pricing for details.

What is the Founding Community?

Our group of early users. Members get early access, can apply for the beta and have a direct say in what we build next.

What's the difference between the waitlist and the beta?

The waitlist gets you an invite when early access opens. The beta is a smaller group that tests features before everyone else and shares feedback with our team. You can choose either one on the sign-up form.

Can my whole organization use MyPM?

Yes. The Team plan is for shared workspaces, and the Command plan is for organizations that need governance across many teams. Talk to us about Command.
