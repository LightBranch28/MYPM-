Pricing questions

Can I change plans later?

Yes. Upgrades are instant. Downgrades apply at the end of your billing cycle, and you keep read-only access to past projects.

Do I need a credit card for the Free plan?

No.

How does Team pricing work?

Per user, per month. Add seats any time.

Who is Command for?

Organizations running many teams and projects. Pricing depends on your size. Contact sales.

Where do I pay?

In the MyPM app, once it launches.
