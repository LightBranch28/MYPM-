Draft. This policy is a placeholder and is waiting for legal review, including which country's law applies. Don't treat it as final.

This policy explains what information MyPM ("we", "us") collects through this website and the MyPM app, how we use it and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us on this website: your name, email address, role, company and any message you send through the early-access form.
  • Account and project information in the app: your profile, workspaces, projects, tasks, milestones, comments and other content you or your team add.
  • Website analytics: if enabled, we measure visits and sign-ups on this website in aggregate, using a privacy-friendly analytics tool.
  • Usage information: basic technical data such as device type, app version and how features are used, so we can keep MyPM reliable and improve it.

How we use it

  • To run the waitlist, beta program and early-access invitations.
  • To provide MyPM's features, including AI analysis of the projects you have access to.
  • To reply to your questions and send updates you've agreed to receive.
  • To keep the service secure and to improve it.

AI and your project data

MyPM's AI only processes project data that the requesting user is allowed to see. AI-suggested changes to deadlines, assignments or other critical fields are never applied without a person's confirmation.

Sharing

We don't sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers who help us run MyPM (for example hosting, database, spam protection, analytics, email, payments and AI processing), under agreements that protect your data, or when the law requires it.

Security

We use encrypted connections, role-based access controls and data isolation between workspaces to protect your information.

Your choices

You can unsubscribe from emails at any time using the link in each email. You can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal information by contacting us.

Contact

For privacy questions, send us a message through the contact form.
